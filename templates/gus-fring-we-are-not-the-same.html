What the Gus Fring we are not the same meme means

Gus Fring from Breaking Bad with the line "We are not the same". It compares two people's habits to make one look superior, often ironically.

How to use it

Describe what "you" do, then what "I" do, and end with "We are not the same". This template has 3 text boxes.
