What the Mocking Spongebob meme means

SpongeBob bent over like a chicken, from a SpongeBob SquarePants episode. It mocks something someone said by repeating it in alternating capital letters.

How to use it

Write the original sentence on top and the same sentence in alternating caps at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.
