What the Look At Me meme means

A pirate from the film Captain Phillips (2013) telling the captain "Look at me. I'm the captain now". It is used when someone suddenly takes charge.

How to use it

Keep "Look at me" on top and write the new role at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.
