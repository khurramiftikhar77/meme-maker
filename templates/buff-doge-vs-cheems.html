What the Buff Doge vs. Cheems meme means

A muscular Shiba Inu dog next to a small, weak one called Cheems. It compares a strong past version of something with a weak present one.

How to use it

Label the top of each dog with what they represent, then write the strong trait under Buff Doge and the weak trait under Cheems. This template has 4 text boxes.
