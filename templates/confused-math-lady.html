What the Confused Math Lady meme means

Brazilian actress Renata Sorrah in a scene from the 2004 soap opera "Senhora do Destino", with geometry and equations floating around her. It stands for trying to work out something that makes no sense.

How to use it

Put the confusing thing in the caption: a job post, a salary range, an org chart or a project plan. Top and bottom text both work. This template has 2 text boxes.

Confused Math Lady caption ideas

  • Me trying to work out / what "we're a family here" means in my payslip
  • Me reading the new org chart / to find out who my boss is
  • Me calculating / how many meetings I can skip without anyone noticing
  • When the job post says "entry level" / and asks for 5 years of experience

Open the Confused Math Lady template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
