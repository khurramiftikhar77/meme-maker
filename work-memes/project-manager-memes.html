Tips for sharing project manager memes

A well-timed meme can make a status update or a retro far more memorable. Keep it light, aim it at the process (scope creep, shifting deadlines, missing updates) and never at a specific colleague or client.

Frequently asked questions

What are project manager memes about?
Deadlines, scope creep, status reports, risk registers, stand-ups, dependencies and the eternal question of who owns the action item.
Is it OK to share these memes at work?
The captions here are written to be work-safe and to joke about situations, not people. As always, you know your team best, so adjust the words to fit.
