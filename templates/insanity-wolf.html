What the Insanity Wolf meme means

A snarling wolf on a red and orange background, used for captions about wild, extreme reactions to normal situations.

How to use it

Write a normal situation on top and an absurdly extreme reaction at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.

Insanity Wolf caption ideas

  • Eats cereal / with orange juice
  • Sleeps 2 hours / goes to the gym twice
  • Charges phone / to 101%
  • Microwaves leftovers / for 40 minutes
  • Reads the ending first / then the beginning

Open the Insanity Wolf template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
