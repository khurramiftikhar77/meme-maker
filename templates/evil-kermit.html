What the Evil Kermit meme means

Kermit the Frog facing his hooded, darker double, from Muppets Most Wanted (2014). It shows the voice of temptation talking you into a bad idea.

How to use it

Write your good intention as "Me" on top and the tempting bad idea as "Also me" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.

Evil Kermit caption ideas

  • Me: I should go to sleep / Also me: watch one more video
  • Me: I will save money / Also me: it is on sale, buy it
  • Me: I will eat healthy / Also me: order the large fries
  • Me: study for the exam / Also me: you can learn it the night before
  • Me: be productive today / Also me: reorganize your playlist

Open the Evil Kermit template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
