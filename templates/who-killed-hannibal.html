What the Who Killed Hannibal meme means

From The Eric Andre Show: Eric shoots Hannibal Buress, then turns to the camera and asks "Who killed Hannibal?". It is about causing a problem and then asking who caused it.

How to use it

Label Eric with who caused it, the gun with what they did, and the last panel with the innocent question. This template has 3 text boxes.
