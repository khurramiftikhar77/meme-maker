What the Yo Dawg Heard You meme means

Rapper Xzibit, host of the TV show Pimp My Ride, laughing. It jokes about putting something inside more of the same thing.

How to use it

Write "Yo dawg, I heard you like [thing]" on top and "so I put [thing] in your [thing]" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.
