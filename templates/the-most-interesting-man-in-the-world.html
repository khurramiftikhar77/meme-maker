What the The Most Interesting Man In The World meme means

Actor Jonathan Goldsmith from a beer advert, known for "I don't always..., but when I do...". It is used for humorous exceptions.

How to use it

Start with "I don't always" on top and finish with "but when I do" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.

The Most Interesting Man In The World caption ideas

  • I do not always eat vegetables / but when I do, they are on a pizza
  • I do not always wake up early / but when I do, I go back to sleep
  • I do not always test my code / but when I do, I do it in production
  • I do not always read the instructions / but when I do, it is after I broke it
  • I do not always go to the gym / but when I do, I post about it

Open the The Most Interesting Man In The World template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
