What the Laughing Leo meme means

Leonardo DiCaprio laughing with a drink in his hand, from Django Unchained. It is used for laughing at someone else's misfortune or an absurd moment.

How to use it

Describe the funny situation across the top and bottom text. This template has 2 text boxes.
