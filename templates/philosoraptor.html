What the Philosoraptor meme means

A velociraptor resting its claw on its chin in deep thought. It asks silly philosophical questions and puns.

How to use it

Write a thoughtful-sounding but silly question across the top and bottom text. This template has 2 text boxes.

Philosoraptor caption ideas

  • If you try to fail and succeed / did you fail or succeed?
  • If I eat myself / do I get twice as big or disappear?
  • If nothing is impossible / is it possible to fail?
  • If money does not grow on trees / why do banks have branches?
  • If you clean a vacuum / are you the vacuum cleaner?

Open the Philosoraptor template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
