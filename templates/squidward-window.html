What the Squidward window meme means

Squidward from "SpongeBob SquarePants" watching through his window as SpongeBob and Patrick have fun outside. It shows being stuck doing something dull while others enjoy themselves.

How to use it

Label Squidward with yourself and what you are stuck doing, and the window with everyone else having fun. This template has 2 text boxes.

Squidward window caption ideas

  • Me in the office on RTO Monday / The colleagues who negotiated full remote
  • Me in the 9am standup / The team in another time zone still asleep
  • Me doing the release / Everyone else at the Christmas party
  • Me writing the documentation / Devs shipping without reading it

Open the Squidward window template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
