What the Sleeping Shaq meme means

Shaquille O'Neal fast asleep in one panel and wide awake in another. It shows ignoring something important and caring a lot about something trivial.

How to use it

Write what you ignore next to sleeping Shaq and what wakes you up next to alert Shaq. This template has 2 text boxes.

Sleeping Shaq caption ideas

  • My alarm going off / and me ignoring it
  • Teacher explaining / me during the lesson
  • Responsibilities calling / me on a Sunday
  • Mom yelling my name / me pretending to be asleep
  • Notifications at night / me wide awake at 3am

Open the Sleeping Shaq template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
