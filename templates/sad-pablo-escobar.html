What the Sad Pablo Escobar meme means

Three stills of Wagner Moura as Pablo Escobar in the series Narcos, sitting and waiting alone and looking bored. It shows loneliness or waiting for something that never comes.

How to use it

Use the three lines to tell a short, sad story that builds up and lets you down. This template has 3 text boxes.
