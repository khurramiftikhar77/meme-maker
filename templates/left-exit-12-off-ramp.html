What the Left Exit 12 Off Ramp meme means

A car swerves across a motorway to take the exit at the last second instead of continuing straight. It is used when someone suddenly picks the worse or more chaotic option.

How to use it

Write the sensible option on the straight-ahead sign, the chaotic option on the exit sign, and label the car with who makes the swerve. This template has 3 text boxes.
