What the That Would Be Great meme means

Bill Lumbergh from the film "Office Space" (1999) holding a coffee mug and making a passive-aggressive request. He is the ultimate symbol of the annoying boss.

How to use it

Write the unreasonable request at the top, starting with "If you could...", and finish with "that would be great" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.
