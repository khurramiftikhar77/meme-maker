What the UNO Draw 25 Cards meme means

A two-panel meme where someone is offered an UNO card saying "do this or draw 25", and in the next panel they are holding a huge stack of cards. It shows someone who would rather suffer than do one simple thing.

How to use it

Write the simple thing they refuse to do on the card, and label the person holding all 25 cards. This template has 2 text boxes.
