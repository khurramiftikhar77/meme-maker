What the Roll Safe Think About It meme means

A man tapping his head with a confident grin, from the 2015 web series #HoodDocumentary. It presents a terrible piece of logic as if it were genius.

How to use it

Write a clever-sounding but obviously flawed "life hack" across the top and bottom text. This template has 2 text boxes.
