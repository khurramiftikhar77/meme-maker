What the X, X Everywhere meme means

Buzz Lightyear with his arm around a worried Woody, from Toy Story, gesturing at the horizon. It is used when something is suddenly all over the place.

How to use it

Put the thing at the top and "[thing] everywhere" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.

What is the X, X Everywhere meme?
Buzz Lightyear with his arm around a worried Woody, from Toy Story, gesturing at the horizon. It is used when something is suddenly all over the place.
How do I make a X, X Everywhere meme?
Click "Make this meme" to open the template in Meme Maker, then click any text on the image to type your own. Put the thing at the top and "[thing] everywhere" at the bottom.
How many text boxes does the X, X Everywhere template have?
It has 2 text boxes. You can add more text, or move, resize and rotate any of it in the editor.
