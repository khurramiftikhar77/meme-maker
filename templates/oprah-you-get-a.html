What the Oprah You Get A meme means

Oprah Winfrey excitedly giving every audience member a car on her 2004 show. It is used when something is handed out to everyone, whether they want it or not.

How to use it

Write "You get a [thing]" on top and "Everybody gets a [thing]" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.

Oprah You Get A caption ideas

  • You get a bug / everybody gets a bug
  • You get a meeting / everybody gets a meeting
  • You get a nap / everybody gets a nap
  • You get homework / everybody gets homework
  • You get a group chat notification / everybody gets one

Open the Oprah You Get A template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
