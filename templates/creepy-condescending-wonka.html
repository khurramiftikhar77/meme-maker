What the Creepy Condescending Wonka meme means

Gene Wilder as Willy Wonka in the 1971 film, smiling with a patronising look. It sarcastically questions someone's claim.

How to use it

Repeat someone's doubtful claim as a question on top and add a sarcastic "tell me more" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.
