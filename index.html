      Frequently asked questions

      Can I post these memes on LinkedIn?
      Yes. Click Post to LinkedIn: MemeLink copies your meme and opens LinkedIn with your post text filled in, so you only paste the image. On phones it opens your share menu so you can pick the LinkedIn app.
      What is the best image size for a LinkedIn meme?
      Square 1200×1200 works everywhere in the feed, and portrait 1080×1350 takes up more space on phones. MemeLink exports both, plus landscape 1200×627, and fills the empty space with a blurred, white or black background.
      Are LinkedIn memes professional?
      Work humour that people relate to, like meetings, deadlines and buzzwords, usually does well on LinkedIn. Keep it about shared experiences rather than mocking a specific person or company.
      Is MemeLink free?
      Yes. MemeLink is completely free, has no watermark and needs no account.
      How do I make a meme?
      Search for a template or upload your own picture. Then click any text on the image to type your own caption, and click Download PNG when you are done.
      Can I use my own picture?
      Yes. Open the Upload picture tab and choose, drag in or paste an image. It is edited on your device and never uploaded to our servers.
      How do I edit the text on a meme?
      Click the text on the image and start typing. Drag it to move it, use the corner dot to resize it, the side dots to change how wide it wraps, and the round dot to rotate it. The Text panel changes the font, size, colours and outline.
      Where do the meme templates come from?
      Templates come from Imgflip, Memegen and the r/MemeTemplatesOfficial community. They belong to their respective owners.
      Does it work on phones?
      Yes. MemeLink works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.
