Tips for sharing developer memes

The best programming memes are specific. Swap in your own framework, tool or ticket number, keep the joke about the situation rather than a teammate, and post it in the channel where everyone lived through the same incident.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a good developer meme?
A shared pain point everyone on the team recognises: flaky tests, Friday deploys, legacy code, endless meetings or a code review that says "LGTM" after four seconds. Keep it about the situation, not a person.
Can I post these memes in Slack or on LinkedIn?
Yes. Download the meme as a PNG or use Copy to paste it straight into Slack, Teams, Discord or a LinkedIn post. There is no watermark.
Which templates work best for programming memes?
Drake Hotline Bling, This Is Fine, Gru's Plan, Expanding Brain and Who Would Win? are favourites because they map neatly onto choices, plans that go wrong and tiny bugs that beat big systems.
