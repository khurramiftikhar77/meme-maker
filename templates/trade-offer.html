What the Trade Offer meme means

A man in a suit pitching a trade with "I receive" on one side and "You receive" on the other. It is used for deals that are clearly one-sided or silly.

How to use it

Write what you get under "I receive" and what the other person gets under "You receive". This template has 2 text boxes.

Trade Offer caption ideas

  • I receive: your fries / You receive: my love
  • I receive: the remote / You receive: a hug
  • I receive: the last slice / You receive: a thank you
  • I receive: your homework / You receive: my friendship
  • I receive: 8 hours of sleep / You receive: a functional me

Open the Trade Offer template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
