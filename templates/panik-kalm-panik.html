What the Panik Kalm Panik meme means

Three panels of a simple drawn face (Meme Man) panicking, calming down, then panicking again. It is used for situations that get worse after a moment of relief.

How to use it

Write the problem, then the relief, then the twist that brings the panic back. This template has 3 text boxes.
