What the Distracted Boyfriend meme means

A stock photo by Antonio Guillem of a man turning to look at a woman in red while his girlfriend glares at him. It became a hit in 2017 as a picture of temptation and being easily distracted.

How to use it

Label the woman in red with the new temptation, the man with who is tempted, and the girlfriend with what they should be paying attention to. This template has 3 text boxes.
