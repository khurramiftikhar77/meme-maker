What the Gigachad meme means

A stylised black-and-white photo of a muscular, square-jawed model, known online as Gigachad. It shows someone confidently doing the sensible thing others are afraid to do.

How to use it

Write the bold action at the top and the calm, confident detail at the bottom, for example "Declines the meeting" and "Without giving a reason". This template has 2 text boxes.
