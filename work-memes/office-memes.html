Tips for sharing office memes

Office memes work because everyone has been there. Pick a moment the whole team recognises, keep it friendly, and post it where it fits: the random channel, a Friday team update or your LinkedIn feed.

What are the most relatable office memes?
Meetings that could have been emails, reply-all chains, video call mishaps, the 4:59pm "got a minute?" and free food in the kitchen. They work in any team and any industry.

Yes. The captions are written to be work-safe and to laugh at situations rather than people, so they fit a team chat or a LinkedIn post.
