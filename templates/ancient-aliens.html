What the Ancient Aliens meme means

Giorgio Tsoukalos with wild hair from the History channel series Ancient Aliens. It jokingly blames aliens for anything that is hard to explain.

How to use it

Describe a mystery in the top text and answer "aliens" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.
