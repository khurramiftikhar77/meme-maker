What the Boardroom Meeting Suggestion meme means

A boss asks a question in a meeting, three employees answer, and the one with the honest answer is thrown out of the window. It mocks bosses who ignore the obvious solution.

How to use it

Write the boss's question, three answers, and leave the last panel for the reaction. This template has 5 text boxes.

Boardroom Meeting Suggestion caption ideas

  • How do we fix the bug? / Add more tests / Read the logs / Delete the feature
