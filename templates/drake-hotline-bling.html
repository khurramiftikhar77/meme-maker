What the Drake Hotline Bling meme means

Two stills of Drake from his 2015 "Hotline Bling" music video: in the top panel he turns away in disgust, in the bottom he points and smiles in approval. It is the internet's standard way to show a preference.

How to use it

Put the thing you reject next to the top panel and the thing you prefer next to the bottom panel. It works best when the "better" option is funnier, lazier or more absurd than the sensible one. This template has 2 text boxes.
