What the Change My Mind meme means

A man sits at an outdoor table behind a sign that states an opinion and invites people to "change my mind". It comes from a 2018 photo of commentator Steven Crowder and is used for strong or silly hot takes.

How to use it

Write your hot take on the sign. The top text is optional. This template has 2 text boxes.
