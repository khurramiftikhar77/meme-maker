What the Two Buttons meme means

A sweating man stares at two red buttons, unable to choose. It comes from a 2014 comic by Jake Clark and is used for any impossible or ridiculous dilemma.

How to use it

Write the two options on the buttons and label the sweating man at the bottom with who is facing the choice. This template has 3 text boxes.
