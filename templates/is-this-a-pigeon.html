What the Is This A Pigeon meme means

An anime character points at a butterfly and asks "Is this a pigeon?". It comes from the 1990s anime The Brave of Sun Fighbird and mocks someone confidently getting something wrong.

How to use it

Label the butterfly with the thing, the man with who is confused, and put the wrong conclusion in the question at the bottom. This template has 3 text boxes.
