      Frequently asked questions

      Is Meme Maker free?
      Yes. Meme Maker is completely free, has no watermark and needs no account.
      How do I make a meme?
      Search for a template or upload your own picture. Then click any text on the image to type your own caption, and click Download PNG when you are done.
      Can I use my own picture?
      Yes. Open the Upload picture tab and choose, drag in or paste an image. It is edited on your device and never uploaded to our servers.
      How do I edit the text on a meme?
      Click the text on the image and start typing. Drag it to move it, use the corner dot to resize it, the side dots to change how wide it wraps, and the round dot to rotate it. The Text panel changes the font, size, colours and outline.
      Where do the meme templates come from?
      Templates come from Imgflip, Memegen and the r/MemeTemplatesOfficial community. They belong to their respective owners.
      Does it work on phones?
      Yes. Meme Maker works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.
