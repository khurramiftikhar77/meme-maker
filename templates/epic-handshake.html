What the Epic Handshake meme means

Two muscular arms meeting in a handshake, from the 1987 film Predator with Arnold Schwarzenegger and Carl Weathers. It shows two different groups that agree on one thing.

How to use it

Label each arm with a group and write what they agree on over the clasped hands. This template has 3 text boxes.
