What the Socially Awkward Penguin meme means

A penguin on a blue background, used for captions about awkward social moments.

How to use it

Describe the awkward situation on top and the cringe result at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.

Socially Awkward Penguin caption ideas

  • Someone says "have a good flight" / "you too"
  • Sees someone I know in the shop / hides behind the cereal
  • Gets a phone call / lets it ring until it stops
  • Someone waves / waves back at the person behind me
  • Rehearses ordering pizza / still says it wrong

Open the Socially Awkward Penguin template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
