What the Anakin Padme 4 Panel meme means

Four stills from Star Wars: Attack of the Clones. Anakin says something, Padme smiles and asks a hopeful follow-up, Anakin stares silently, and Padme repeats the question nervously.

How to use it

Give Anakin a plan, give Padme a hopeful question, leave the third panel empty, and repeat Padme's question in the fourth. This template has 4 text boxes.
