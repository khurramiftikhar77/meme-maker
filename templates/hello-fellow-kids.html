What the Hello Fellow Kids meme means

Steve Buscemi in a 2012 episode of "30 Rock", dressed as a teenager with a skateboard and a backwards cap, asking "How do you do, fellow kids?". It mocks brands and bosses trying too hard to seem young and cool.

How to use it

Write the out-of-touch line at the top and the cringe detail at the bottom. Corporate culture posts and CEO TikTok attempts are perfect targets. This template has 2 text boxes.
