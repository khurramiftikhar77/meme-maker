What the Chill Guy meme means

A cartoon dog in a grey sweater and jeans, hands in pockets, smirking in a field. Drawn by artist Phillip Banks in 2023, it went viral in late 2024 as the face of staying calm while everything around you is chaos.

How to use it

Describe the stressful situation at the top and label Chill Guy at the bottom with whoever stays relaxed. It is perfect for LinkedIn posts about deadlines, reorgs and Monday mornings. This template has 2 text boxes.

Chill Guy caption ideas

  • Everyone panicking about the reorg / Me, a chill guy who updated his CV in March
  • Prod is down and the CEO joined the channel / Me, a chill guy who is on holiday
  • Deadline moved up by 2 weeks / Me, a chill guy with 0 tasks in Jira
  • When the recruiter asks for my salary expectations / Just a chill guy asking for 30% more

Open the Chill Guy template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
