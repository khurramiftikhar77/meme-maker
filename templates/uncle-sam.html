What the Uncle Sam meme means

The classic Uncle Sam recruitment poster pointing at the viewer with "I want you". It turns any request into an urgent call to action.

How to use it

Keep "I want you" on top and write what you want people to do at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.

Uncle Sam caption ideas

  • I want you / to reply to the group chat
  • I want you / to stop leaving dishes in the sink
  • I want you / to write tests
  • I want you / to drink some water
  • I want you / to go to bed

Open the Uncle Sam template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Uncle Sam meme?
The classic Uncle Sam recruitment poster pointing at the viewer with "I want you". It turns any request into an urgent call to action.
How do I make a Uncle Sam meme?
Click "Make this meme" to open the template in Meme Maker, then click any text on the image to type your own. Keep "I want you" on top and write what you want people to do at the bottom.
How many text boxes does the Uncle Sam template have?
It has 2 text boxes. You can add more text, or move, resize and rotate any of it in the editor.
