What the It's Not Possible, No It's Necessary meme means

A scene from the film "Interstellar" (2014): one panel says "It's not possible", and Matthew McConaughey answers "No. It's necessary." It shows someone doing the impossible because they have no choice.

How to use it

Write the impossible demand in the top panel and the reply in the bottom panel, like a 3-month project due in 2 weeks: "No. It's in the roadmap." This template has 2 text boxes.
