What the Gru's Plan meme means

Four panels of Gru from Despicable Me presenting a plan on a flip chart. In the last panel he looks back at the chart in shock because the final step has gone wrong.

How to use it

Fill the first three boards with steps of a plan, then repeat the third step on the fourth board so it looks like Gru only now realises the flaw. This template has 4 text boxes.
