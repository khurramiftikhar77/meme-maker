Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Meme Maker (https://mememaker.khurramiftikhar.com) handles when you use it, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email contact@khurramiftikhar.com.

Pictures and text you add

Meme Maker works in your browser. Pictures you upload, drag in or paste, and the text you write, are processed on your device. We do not upload them to our servers and we do not keep a copy.

The one exception is the AI features. If you use caption ideas from Claude or the Ask Claude tab, a smaller copy of the image on screen, the text in your text boxes, and any idea you type are sent through our server to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, so it can write suggestions. Caption ideas for images without built-in captions are requested automatically when you open them. We do not store this content. Anthropic handles it under its own privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Meme template sources

When you search templates, your browser loads lists and images directly from Imgflip, Memegen and Reddit, and some images through the images.weserv.nl image service. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These services receive your IP address and normal browser information when your browser contacts them, under their own privacy policies.

Hosting, security and limits

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. To prevent overuse of the AI features, we count AI requests per IP address for up to 24 hours. We do not use this information for anything else.

Advertising and cookies

Meme Maker is free because it shows ads served by Google AdSense. We do not set our own cookies, but Google and its partners do:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time through the privacy message on the site.

Children

Meme Maker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Because we do not keep your pictures, text or an account for you, there is usually nothing for us to delete. For anything held by Google, Anthropic or Cloudflare, use their tools or contact them directly. You can also email us and we will help where we can.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
