What the Absolute Cinema meme means

Director Martin Scorsese raising both hands as if presenting a masterpiece, a meme that took off in 2024. It is used to praise something as perfect, often ironically.

How to use it

Write the "masterpiece" in the caption. It works ironically (a 3-hour all-hands) and sincerely (a job post that includes the salary). This template has 2 text boxes.

Absolute Cinema caption ideas

  • A PowerPoint with 84 slides / and the conclusion on slide 84
