What the Two Paths meme means

A cartoon of a boy standing where a path splits into a sunny road to a castle and a dark, stormy road. It shows choosing between the good option and the tempting bad one.

How to use it

Label the bright path, the dark path and the boy at the bottom with who has to choose. This template has 3 text boxes.
