What the Surprised Pikachu meme means

Pikachu from the Pokémon anime with its mouth wide open in shock. It mocks people who are surprised by the obvious result of their own actions.

How to use it

Write the cause and its obvious result in the top lines, then let Pikachu's face do the rest. This template has 3 text boxes.
