What the Always Has Been meme means

Two astronauts look at Earth. One realises something ("Wait, it's all...?") while the other, holding a gun behind him, replies "Always has been". It is used for a surprising truth that was obvious all along.

How to use it

Start the top text with "Wait, it's all..." and keep "Always has been" for the second astronaut. This template has 2 text boxes.
