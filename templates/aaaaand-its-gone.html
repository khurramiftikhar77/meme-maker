What the Aaaaand Its Gone meme means

A bank clerk from South Park saying "Aaaand it's gone" as money vanishes. It is used for anything that disappears instantly.

How to use it

Describe what you had on top and keep "aaaaand it's gone" at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.
