What the Bell Curve meme means

An IQ bell curve with a simple character on the left, an anxious overthinker in the middle and a hooded wise figure on the right. Both ends agree on the simple answer while the middle overcomplicates it. Also called the midwit meme.

How to use it

Put the same simple answer on both ends and the overcomplicated version in the middle, for example "Just send an email" vs. "3 alignment meetings and a working group". This template has 3 text boxes.
