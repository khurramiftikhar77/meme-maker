What the Marked Safe From meme means

A parody of a social network safety check notice that marks someone as safe from an event. It jokes about avoiding something mildly unpleasant.

How to use it

Keep "Marked safe from" on top and write the thing you avoided at the bottom. This template has 2 text boxes.
