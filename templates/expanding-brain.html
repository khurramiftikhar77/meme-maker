What the Expanding Brain meme means

Four panels with a brain that grows brighter and bigger at each step. It ranks ideas from "normal" to "enlightened", usually ironically, so the most ridiculous idea gets the biggest brain.

How to use it

Write four ideas in the left column, from most sensible to most absurd. This template has 4 text boxes.
