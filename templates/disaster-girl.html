What the Disaster Girl meme means

A photo of a young girl smiling knowingly at the camera while a house burns behind her, taken in the mid-2000s. It suggests someone quietly responsible for chaos.

How to use it

Use the top and bottom text to hint at the disaster she caused, ideally something small that went very wrong. This template has 2 text boxes.

Disaster Girl caption ideas

  • They said / it was just a small kitchen fire
  • I only microwaved / a fork for a second
  • When someone says / have fun at the office party
  • Group project / after my part
  • I just said / let me fix it real quick

Open the Disaster Girl template in the editor to use one of these or write your own.
