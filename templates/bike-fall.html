What the Bike Fall meme means

Three panels of a cyclist putting a stick into his own front wheel and then lying on the ground blaming someone else. It is about causing your own problems.

How to use it

Label the cyclist, write the self-sabotaging action on the stick, and put the complaint in the last panel. This template has 3 text boxes.
