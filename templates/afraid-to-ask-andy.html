What the Afraid To Ask Andy meme means

Andy Dwyer (Chris Pratt) from Parks and Recreation admitting he doesn't know something "and at this point I'm too afraid to ask". It is about pretending to understand something for too long.

How to use it

Write what you never understood on top and keep the "too afraid to ask" ending. This template has 2 text boxes.

Afraid To Ask Andy caption ideas
