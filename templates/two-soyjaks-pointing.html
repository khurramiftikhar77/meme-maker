What the Two Soyjaks Pointing meme means

Two bearded "soyjak" drawings with open mouths pointing excitedly at something. It mocks people who get overly excited about anything new, shiny or on-trend.

How to use it

Write who is excited at the top and what they are pointing at below. In work memes it fits investors seeing "AI" or managers seeing a dashboard. This template has 2 text boxes.
