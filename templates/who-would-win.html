What the Who Would Win? meme means

A side-by-side comparison asking "Who would win?" between something huge or powerful and something tiny. The joke is that the tiny thing always wins.

How to use it

Put the big, impressive thing on the left and the small thing that defeats it on the right. This template has 2 text boxes.

Who Would Win? caption ideas

  • A 10,000 line codebase / One missing semicolon
  • My diet plan / One free donut
  • 8 hours of sleep / One notification
  • A whole army / One Lego on the floor
  • My willpower / A sale sign
