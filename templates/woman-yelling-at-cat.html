What the Woman Yelling At Cat meme means

Two pictures combined in 2019: a tearful, shouting woman from The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills, and Smudge the cat sitting at a dinner table looking confused. It shows an angry accusation and a calm, ridiculous reply.

How to use it

Write the accusation over the woman on the left and the cat's smug or clueless answer on the right. This template has 2 text boxes.
