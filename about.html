About Meme Maker

Meme Maker is a free online meme generator. It lets anyone turn a picture into a meme in a few seconds, right in the browser, with nothing to install and no account to create.

What you can do

  • Search more than 500 popular meme templates, collected from Imgflip, Memegen and the r/MemeTemplatesOfficial community.
  • Upload, drag in or paste your own picture.
  • Click any text on the image to edit it, add as many text boxes as you like, and move, resize or rotate them.
  • Pick from ready-made caption ideas for popular templates, with text already placed in the right spots, or use general ideas for any other picture.
  • Change the font, size, fill and outline colors, then download your meme as a PNG, copy it, or share it.

Privacy first

Pictures you upload are edited on your own device and never sent to our servers. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
